# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors are expected to keep discussions respectful, specific, and focused on improving the project. Safety-sensitive disagreements should be handled with evidence, reproduction steps, and clear risk language.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment, threats, or discriminatory comments
- publishing private information without permission
- intentionally misleading claims about real-hardware verification
- pressuring contributors to bypass safety checks

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior harms contributors or project safety. Reports should include links, screenshots, and enough context for review.
